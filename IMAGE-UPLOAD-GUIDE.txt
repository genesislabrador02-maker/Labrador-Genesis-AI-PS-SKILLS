IMAGE UPLOAD GUIDE

1. FIRST PAGE / HERO
Replace images/hero.png with your own TRANSPARENT PNG portrait.
Keep the filename: hero.png

2. SECOND PAGE / PROFILE
Replace images/profile.png with your own TRANSPARENT PNG portrait.
Keep the filename: profile.png

Important: PNG must have a transparent background. If the photo still has a rectangular background, that rectangle will appear on the website because it is part of the image itself.

The HTML/CSS image slots have NO border, box, frame, background, or overlay.
